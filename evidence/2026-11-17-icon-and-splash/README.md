## What the build puts in the APK

```
$ unzip -l bin/portfwd-0.3.0-arm64-v8a-debug.apk | grep -E "mipmap|presplash"
     44593  res/drawable/presplash.jpg
       448  res/mipmap-anydpi-v26/icon.xml
     98770  res/mipmap/icon.png
      1174  res/mipmap/icon_background.png
     94622  res/mipmap/icon_foreground.png
```

`res/mipmap-anydpi-v26/icon.xml` is the adaptive icon android 8+ resolves;
`res/mipmap/icon.png` is what API 24/25 use. `icon-renditions.png` shows the
three renditions side by side as the launcher composes them: the adaptive pair
inside a squircle mask, the legacy bitmap with the Android 13 legacy treatment
(72/108 on a light background) and the raw legacy bitmap.

## Notes

- The artwork's plate is a circle (centre 899.5,999.5, radius 860 in the 2x
  render). The adaptive foreground is masked to exactly that circle so its
  white corners can never paint over the background layer, and the background
  layer is the plate's colour at the adaptive mask edge (`#E7F4EC`).
- The presplash is a real JPEG: python-for-android copies it to
  `drawable/presplash.jpg` without converting, and the view is drawn with
  `FIT_CENTER`, so `android.presplash_color = #FFFFFF` fills the bands around
  the artwork with the same white as the artwork's own margin.
- The first attempt passed the artwork at its native 1800x2000 aspect, so
  `FIT_CENTER` scaled the plate to 96 % of the screen width and the splash
  looked like a full-screen image. The canvas is now 1080x1920 (16:9) with the
  plate at 55 % of its width, which is width-constrained on every portrait
  phone and therefore always leaves a margin.
- The splash screenshot is a raw `adb exec-out screencap` taken ~1 s after
  `am start`, while Kivy and RNS were still importing.
